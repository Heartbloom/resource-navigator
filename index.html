How it works
1
You describe your situation. Household size, income range, urgency, what's going on. Nothing is stored or tied to your identity.
2
We read every program's actual eligibility requirements — not just the category label — and evaluate each one against what you told us.
3
You get a ranked list with the most likely matches first, plus a call script for each one — the actual words to say so you don't have to figure it out on the phone.
4
The list stays current because people who've called these programs share what they found — intake status, wait times, who's actually being accepted right now.
